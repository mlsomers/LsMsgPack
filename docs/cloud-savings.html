This is an estimate, not an invoice forecast. It applies one benchmark's reduction to all the traffic you entered and leaves out request and header overhead, compression CPU, storage, request charges, reserved or committed-use discounts and region differences. The list prices are approximate on-demand prices of US regions and may be out of date: check your provider's pricing calculator or your bill, and enter your own price. The benchmark timings come from a tight loop on one machine; real services spend more time per byte (cache misses, allocations, garbage collection), so profile your own service and benchmark your own data before budgeting.
